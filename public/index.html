Trovious' Library

A two-page (if you count the map) fantasy table-top role-playing adventure for four third level characters, compatible with D&D fifth edition, exploring the library of a wizard who has accidentally trapped himself in vase.

Only Human

My attempt to make a simple table top role-playing game for telling sci-fi stories about competent people investigating dangerous situations on the fringes of an optimistic future.

It's for games centred on discovery rather than violence. It's for stories about struggling against a universe that was not made for us.
